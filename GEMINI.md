# Mushaf Qiyam (مصحف القيام)

Offline voice-tracked hands-free Quran display for Qiyam, Tahajjud, and Tarawih prayers.

- **Stack:** Android Native (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Sherpa-onnx C++ NDK, Zipformer2-CTC).
- **Collaboration Mode:** Governed by [COLLABORATION.md](./COLLABORATION.md) and [.agent/rules/studio-collaboration.md](./.agent/rules/studio-collaboration.md).
